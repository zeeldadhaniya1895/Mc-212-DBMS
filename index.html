MC 212 DBMS Handbook

A self-contained study handbook for database design, from drawing your first entity to producing a complete relational schema with primary keys, foreign keys, and SQL. Every topic is taught with diagrams, worked examples, and the specific mistakes students make most often.

The learning path

Database design happens in three stages. This handbook covers the first two, in order: first decide what to store, then decide how it becomes tables.

Stage 1 · Section 1
Conceptual design
Model entities, attributes, and relationships as an ER/EER diagram.
→
Stage 2 · Section 2
Logical design
Convert the diagram into relations, keys, and constraints.
→
Stage 3
Physical design
Indexes, storage, and tuning. Not covered in these sections.

How to study with this handbook
  1. Read Section 1 in order, and draw every example yourself before looking at the figure.
  2. Try the Section 1 practice problems. Keep your diagrams, because Section 2 converts them.
  3. Read Section 2, then map your own practice diagrams and check them against the solutions.
  4. Before an exam, revise from the quick-revision cards above and the two mistakes chapters.
